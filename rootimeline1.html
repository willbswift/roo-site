The Ur-Universe

-400,000,000,000?             (before the beginning of all beginnings / before the Cataclysm / before the Universe began / before Time / 400,000,000,000 years before the Eye of Harmony?)
                The Ur-Universe exists and expanded. The Ur-Universe became the ancestor-universe of all the subesquent universes. Only two forces existed - Good (light / positive) and Evil (dark / negative).  At this point there were no names for these forces. The Time Lords, including the Doctor (up until The Satan Pit), incorrectly believed it was impossible for any life to exist at this point. 

-50,000,000,000      (50 billion years before Modern Gallifrey / before Time, Light, Space, and Matter / before the Doctor's Universe)  
                The Cataclysm: The Ur-Universe is "open" and expands until heat death and beyond. Space-time became locally flat, with no matter or gravity. The Disciples of Light feared that the legions of the Beast would bring night to the universe. So they fought a massive battle with all Evil, in the form of the Beast. The Disciples might have been the blue-skinned Pwccm. According to legend, they provided the Beast with poisoned food in the form of the Bitter Pill, which it ate.  
                The Ur-Universe's superstrings had a deep-foam structures. Within those structure random interactions eventually create several acausal events where parity is invalidated. The battle between the Disciples of Light and the Beast created the first Big Bang. This creates massive amounts of matter or anti-matter. Time was born as was matter and space and life. The forces of Good and Evil shattered and largely negating each other, leaving only echoes.  In the aftermath of the Cataclysm, these acausal events budded numerous secondary universes in their own bubbles, producing a sea of isolated multiverses billions of light years apart. These multiverses in turn bud their own universes (probably as the acts of deliberate creation by sentient beings). The Ur-Universe becomes like a series of Russian dolls. Inside each universe was another one. Lifeforms, called Intercreationals, were composed of these universes and existed in this ur-space sea of budded universes. These Swimmers were far more powerful than Chronovores (and Guardians) and could destroy universes by touching them. 

-50,000,000,000?      (afterwards the Cataclysm)
                    The world of Krop Tor (Bitter Pill) was the home of a civilization that lived in a vast underground city that was 10 miles beneath the surface. The Disciples of Light had the Beast imprisoned in the Pit beneath the underground city. Sometime between the Time of Legend (when the Time Lords invented black holes) and the Humanian Era, the planet Krop Tor would be suspended by a gravity field over the black hole K-37 Gem 5. The gravity funnel responsible for this suspension required levels of power that inverted with a self-extrapolating reflex of 6 to the 6th power every six seconds. The black hole it orbited was said by some to be alive and that it had tried to consume Krop Tor many times. The Beast had a son named Abaddon who was eventually trapped beneath the Cardiff Rift.

The Multiverse

--             The Third Multi/Universe (which might mean it was the third to bud off from the Omniverse) comes into existance. This multiverse exists in a state of quantum superposition.
            This Universe is constantly degenerating and regenerating in the same way that the Timeless Child would regenerate. The universe expands and contracts with each of these cycles. This process involves an exchange (or reversal) of all matter and antimatter as well as all energies and forces. The speed of this regeneration is governed by the Balance between the forces of Law/Order and Chaos/Creativity, Life and Death, Matter and Antimatter. This Balance is tied to the intelligence and reasoning of sapient beings of the multiverse. This universal regeneration is perpetual and proceeds so slowly that most sapient species are completely unaware of it. But those that can detect it call this parthenogenetic process the Great ReCreation or the Conjunction of the Million Spheres.
            After many cycles of Big Bangs and collapses, this Third Universe will become the Prime Normal-Space Universe 5556 depicted in most Doctor Who stories. The Anchoring of the Eye of Harmony and other actions taken by the Time Lords during the Time of Legend retroactively impose rationality and science onto the Third Universe, creating an actual Retro-History that exists above the Priorverse of pre-history and pre-time. It is possible that the multiverse's superpostion could be violently collapsed, turning the multiverse into a single crystalized web of time.

--            The Fourth Multi/Universe to decend from the Omniuniverse didn't run parallel to the Third. Instead, its time time tracks crossed at an angle. The planet Quinnis could be found in the Fourth Universe. These secondary universes could be accessed using charged vacuum embodiments (CVEs). While naturally occurring CVE were one of the rarest phenomenon in the Prime Universe, hidden artificially created CVE portholes were more common.

All Time: The Eleven Dimensional Universe

Minkowski Space is made of three spatial (dimensions 1, 2, and 3) and two temporal dimensions (4 and 5). The 4th Dimension of Time alows the creation of matter in much the same way that matter would creates gravity. The Weak Nuclear Force allows the existence of the 5th Dimension. This is the relative dimension of Inner Space-Time where the Space-Time Vortex is found (originally known as the Astral Plane). 

Without time travel to interfere, time moves as a harmonious and elegant parabola. The area between the fourth and fifth dimensions is known as the Second Ether (aka Chaos Space, the Shift, the Chaos Realm). The Second Ether is the source of all the creative beauty and entropic horror in the multiverse. According to legend the Doctor was the one who gave the Second Ether its name. The Doctor always makes a point of denying this. The Arc of Infinity is an area that exists between some dimensions.

The last 6 dimensions are curled into a space one thousandth the size of a proton. This Calabai-Yau Space will becomes the Six-Fold Realm of the Guardians. One of these dimensions is the relative dimension of Imagination. Imagination can accessed from Normal Space if one can reach the eye of the storm of the Big Bang, known as the Point of Stillness. Another is probably the relative dimension of Possibility. The quantum Possibility Tree begins to grow. The song of Time moving through Space plays from this tree. The realities of these other dimensions can manifest themselves in the Normal-Space universe as works of fiction and ionic energy can convert fiction into reality or reality into fiction.

The Key to Time


The Key to Time

The Grace designed the Key to Time to allow a Guardian of Time to unlock the barrier around the Heart of the Universe so that it can be changed and reset the Cosmic Balance. This makes it the most important object in Creation. In theory Guardians of Chaos and Structure are perfectly balanced, but if one started to gain an advantage over the other then the Universe would begin to come apart. The Key to Time was designed by the Grace to re-balance things when this happens. The Guardians can use the complete Key to maintain universal balance between order and chaos. It serves as a moral fail-safe to restset the balance of good and evil. 

The Key is created out of a star that would have otherwise developed a planet whose inhabitants would have created an empire of harmony. The Key is created out of a nearly indestructible artificially matricized structure consisting of a substance with a variable atomic weight that borders on magical. This abstract material is forged in the Chaos Pool found on the planet Chaos in the last 6 minutes of the Universe's life-span. The material embodies the fundamental nature of the universe and exists simultaneously at every point in time. This property prevents is from being analyzed. The substance is sometimes used by the Guardians to fashion communicators for lower lifeforms as well as the Crystal of Kronos and Enlightenment. Enlightenment could be used to alter time streams and the events within them. The Key is a perfect cube (6x6x6) made of six parts and holds the elemental force of the Universe. Each Guardian is represented by one segment and together, the Key represents the powers of the Six-Fold God (aka the six Guardians). If the Key was damaged, that damaged would limit the powers of the Guardians. Only when the Key is fully assembled can the Grace safely manifest in Normal-Space, within the Key.

These six segments maintain the equilibrium of Time itself. Any being that has the complete Key has absolute power over every particle in the Universe.  It can stop and start the Universe, re-write matter, change the states of quanta, and alter the balance of good and evil throughout the Universe. The Key gives the user access to every event in history, and the ability to alter those events. The Key might have been some form of Time Manipulator. Only the Prime Eye of Harmony on Gallifrey is its equal when it comes to manipulating the power of the Vortex. It can easily open gateways between universes. While the sixth segment is the most important of the set, any single segment of the Key to Time has the power of transmutation, transformation, and navigating through time and hyperspace. If one has 5 of the 6 segments a temporary segment can be made out of specific materials. Chronodyne is 74% compatible and can be used (with the other 5 segments) to put the whole Universe into a 3 second time-loop for 3.25 minutes before the Chronodyne burns out.

To prevent any one being from having this much power the six segments of the Key are disguised and scattered to specific points in space and time. the segments chance space-time location and coordinates from time to time. This happens often enough that every object or person in the Universe has been disguised as a segment at one point or another. A branch from the Universe’s first tree is turned into the first Tracer. Carved on it are symbols that event he Pantheon of Gods don’t understand. The Key to Time is eventually destroyed by being evaporated in the Chaos Pool 300 years before the start of the Last Great Time War in heaven. This might have been what allowed the Time War to occur.

All Time: The Six-Fold God

The Grace are probably huge and pan-dimensional elemental beings with amazing, inconceivable, powers. The Six-Fold God is made up the Council of Guardians. There are six of these hyper-dimensional Guardians of Time: The White Guardian, the Black Guardian, the Red Guardian, the Crystal Guardian, the Azure Guardian and the Gold Guardian. This is why the Grace us always referred to in the plural. The Grace lives outside the Universe's first 5 dimensions in the howling void of the Six-Fold Realm (aka Calabai-Yau Space). This realm is dispersed throughout eternity but can be reached via the Chaos Pool found at the end of the Universe. However the Guardians find the manifesting in the first 5 dimensions to be very cramped and uncomfortable.

The Guardians must perform all large actions in unison, otherwise cosmic balance will be upset. In general terms, the Grace spends all of their time keeping the Universe in order. They serve as conduits through which the fundamental essence of the Universe can act and are essential for cosmic balance. If the Universe was to end the Guardians would die as well.

The Guardians nurtured the early civilizations of the universe, and one of their jobs is to ensure that the Time Lords do not abuse their abilities to manipulate the Weak Nuclear Force. The Guardians know there will come a time when they are no longer needed and that their existence will end. Eventually they will give their lives, powers, and energy to two of their Tracers named Abby and Zara.

Each Guardian is granted a dimension to serve as their domain. Due to rules created in the Universe's childhood, they can only survive outside the sliver of their dimension for about four weeks. As the Guardians get further from Event 1 their life-span is pushed back, effectively making it endless.

Among the Time Lords, only those who have served (or are serving) as President know of the true nature of the Guardians. When the Grace does reach into reality they fight the can only be perceived by the lesser species as a subtle feeling of misgiving. When interacting with the first 3 dimensions, each Guardian's power waxes and wanes depending on how well their specific traits are dominating the life-forms of the cosmos. In most cases, the Guardians can only directly contact lesser species when the primate's brain has been knocked into an altered state. But it is impossible for any species to really grasp or understand anything about how the Grace thinks and why they do the things they do.

The Gallifreyan Empire

Shobogan culture is similar to Earth's Hellenic Greece period and Old High Gallifreyan has several letters that are similar to Greek. The Gallifreyan Empire focuses most of its energies on exploring undeveloped planets. The Gallifreyans convince most alien races to view them as gods. They become known as the Space Lords of Gallifrey.

The military of the Space Lords is organized into battalions. Space Lords/Officers generally wear red and gold armored uniforms, often with robes. Modern Time Lord ceremonial collars are actually imitations of the battle collars worn by ancient Gallifreyans when going into combat. These are made of a metal-marble compound and inscribed with the seal of the Great Houses. They wear warrior armor with a pointed skullcap and often carry a two-handed bejewelled sword shaped like a massive machete. Soldiers wear armor with a deep red color. The Generals plan their strategies from the War Council room located beneath the Capitol. Commanders have the right to execute their officers and troops if they see fit.  

The Empire is governed by a Pythia. It is believed that the Menti Celesti placed the Pythia in charge of all Shobogans. The Pythia is the Guardian, Embodiment, and Crown of the Gallifreyan Empire. She is also The Symbol of the planet's fertility, Mouthpiece of the Gods, and Guardian of the Great Book of Future Legends. The Pythia is addressed as ‘Your Excellency’ and she might have had the title Majestrix. The Constitution states that the Pythia IS Gallifrey. It also requires that each Pythia name her successor. Traditionally most of the Pythia’s were appointed from the same family. All security systems on Gallifrey can be overridden and controlled by the Pythia. Amulets and talisman hang from golden chains on the Pythia’s robe. The Pythia has a personal physician. Attendants of the Pythian Order wear rust red robes and most have red hair. Wandering Seers can be found traveling from town to town. Pythian seers eat fish tongues. 

Prayer and devotions take place in the temple in the evening. Petitioners (most wanting miracles) wait in the temple court yard. Men are expected to wear fur coats with deep hoods that obscure their faces when entering the Hall of the Gods and having an audience with the Pythia. The Adepts say to a petitioner (who is granted an audience with the Pythia) “Clean let the hearts be of each Seeker.”  The petitioner replies with “So shall we never doubt.” A copper sanctum gate leads to the inner temple. Under the adytum of Temple is the Cavern of Memories. There are granite walls and pillars in this sanctum, with steps leading to the Adytum. One of the pillars is hollow so that the Pythia's bodyguard can hide, waiting to deal with threats. 

To see the future, the Pythia sits in a wicker cage, suspended over the Crevasse of Memories That Will Be. The smoky vapors allow her to achieve the state of clairvoyant and the state of clairaudiant. The Pythia can see the past by gazing into transparent star glass. In this era the irrational force of Odic Energy was plentiful. Odic Energy made miraculous healing and acts of prophecy possible. There are soothsayers who try to read a person’s future in tea leaves. Soothsayers and wizards dispense magick crystals, herbs, spells, and equations to their customers. Humanoids exposed to volcanic gases emitted by a future space-time crack can produce prophecies of the short term future with a great deal of accuracy. Longer term visions are also possible. They can read the memories of a person they make eye contact with. However they will be blind to any event directly triggered by the actual advent of the space-time crack. It is likely that all of this applies to the Pythia. There are weak-points in space time that seem to be created by psionic effects of worship. When time sensitives interact with these small ruptures they can become full blown time fissures. 

The Shobogans see their heritage as the Bright Past and their future is guided by the Pythian Book of Future Legends. The ‘Hand of Osuda’ the hollowed hand of Fate. Virtually everyone believes in, and prays to, the gods know as the Menti Celesti (heavenly minds). These Gods include Time, Death, and Pain. Sacrifices (to regain the favor of the goddesses and for other reasons) are made on alters. Even Shobogans are sometimes sacrificed in the Temple. Curtained alcoves framed with woven wood and flowers serve as Shrines. Votive gifts and offerings are placed on there to gain favor with the Menti Celesti. In times of crisis the Shobogans pray to the Menti Celesti for salvation, But most agree that the goddesses only observe and do not interfere. Ancient Gallifreyans belive that Evil can be used to repel Evil. 

Religious-military imagery of the Time of Chaos includes armored cherubs and archways that don’t actually lead anywhere. The Moon Pazithi is named after Pazithi the mystic virgin moon goddess. Pazithi will still be worshipped at the time of the revolution, despite the moon being covered in mining colonies. The Amphisbaena is a terrible beast described in the Book of Future Legends. An ancient Gallifreyan prophacy predicted the eventual coming of a hybrid of two warring species. This hybrid was destined to conquer Gallifrey, stand among its ruins, and unravel the Web of Time.

The Court of Principals governs under the Pythia. The Court has a Council made up of 100 Councilors (which includes at least one Cardinal). The Councilors of this court form a meritocracy. Prelates control individual villages. Gallifrey has a population in the millions at this point. The nobles of Gallifrey have estates in South Gallifrey. Most of the power was held by the matronymic dynasties of the Great Houses.

The Council Police (aka the Chancellery Watch) continues to act as law enforcement. The Council Police receives most of the funding from the Temple Welfare Trust. This helps ensure loyalty to the Pythia. People who are convicted of minor crimes work at the suet factories. People who are stoned  have their heads put on pikes. 

The Mythic Schools teach the children. They have a very ‘one size fits all’ approach to education and emphesize religious teachings. Older Gallifreyans are educated at the Academia which is located in the City's Olmesian Quarter. The Academia is controlled by the Court of Principals. The core curriculum focuses on Cosmic Science, while items related to the rest of the Universe's history, species, and cultures is considered to be General Studies. This includes philosophy and strategy. Academia Endurance Courses cover sports, swordplay, and military maneuvers. Cadets memorize the Book of Future Legends as part of their training. Practicals are given to test a student’s knowledge of a subject. Students are called cadets and live in barrack rooms. Military Cadets are called Young Heroes. Military Officers and Generals are called Heroes. 

Some Shobogans could, with extensive training, be taught to shield their minds and thoughts from others. Others are born knowing how to do this instinctively. In either case these Shobogans are known as Individuals. The Individuals were considered, by some, to be a cult. Children who were natural born Individuals instinctively know how to navigate the Astral Vortex. But almost all of these children forget how to do this as they become adults. All of Gallifrey's Heroes and great philosophers were Individuals.  

Gallifreyans have abolished racism. Currency includes gold coins, treazants, depaks, and dumplings. A few Treazants can buy a cheap slave. Traders traditionally wear furs. They belive that their art transcends the imagination of lesser species. Shobogans from this time use ivory as a decorative material. Healers wear white tunics with red jerkins. The Gallifreyans speak and write Old High Gallifreyan (10,000,000 letters in alphabet), and Middle Gallifreyan. Vox Relators are the closest thing to a free press, but very little emphasis is made on recording and documenting Gallifrey's history. Instead, they focus almost entirely on documenting the explorations and conquests of other worlds. It is unthinkable to marry at 16. The bodies of the dead were usualy burned in a pyre.

OLD TIME: PLANETS OF EVIL

Omega designed planetary containment spheres made of forced-matter to be created around these HEM node points. The barrier between Normal Space and the Anti-Universe looks like a completely opaque back lake or pool. 

The mutagenic material is hidden beneath at approximately 20 miles of forced-matter rock. These shells were designed to look like planets on the outside. These artificial planets are seeded with a stock set of plants and animals, such as rats, herbivorous reptiles, and spiders. Since the Earth has a Well of the Soul, finding plants or animals that are the same as those found on the Earth can be an indicator that a world holds a well. 

All the dimensional tears are never really completely healed, merely capped/plugged in a way that makes it impossible for the Yssgaroth to pass through. Other species from both universes (including Normal Space Time Lords) can be pulled through if the right technology is applied to one side or the other. If someone was to drill more than 20 miles into the forced-matter crust over one of these node points, they would find a bright green slime that radiates incredible amounts of heat. This mutagen might be related to the Stahlman's gas that Project Inferno was looking for.

Rather than matter, the ante-matter found in the Anti-Universe was composed of an alter-time state that is a type of nothingness itself. This corruption to space-time doesn’t follow the laws of thermodynamics in Normal Space and will never cool down. Proximity to the material appears to make it easy for even the most rudimentary of time travel capsules to slip into a parallel universe, and back again. This could be because all the dimensional tears connect to each other. So if you find one, you can use it to visit the others. As with almost all things vampiric, the light of a Normal Space star will repel and weaken any anti-energy that might escape from the hole. The ante-matter is infectious and mutagenic. Infection is caused by anti-quarks penetrating the subject. Matter that it touches the slime will become corrupted and that matter will often take days to cool down. 

Organic cells that come in contact with it will suffer mutagenic retrogression. Infected life-forms from Normal Space will become ante-beings that feed on the life-force of other life. The ante-matter hybridizes the genetic structure, regressing life-forms down their evolutionary line. Humans exposed to the pure mutagen are turned into vicious fur covered primord animals in two or three hours. Large amounts of exposure and/or extreme heat accelerate this process. Zero particles (whether in gaseous or liquid form) can act as an Anti-Quark Vaccine and temporarily undo the mutational effects. But eventually the hybridization reaches the point where the metabolic changes become permanent. These primords are scaldingly hot. Sometimes their eyes glow red. Mutated individuals can absorb accelerated neutrons and use them to create deadly non-corporeal extensions of themselves made of pure ante-matter. In the right circumstances, they are even able to synthesize new ante-matter crystals in their bodies. 

The HEM is designed to coat and seal the contamination from the rest of the universe. It’s possible that, over time, this process is what is responsible for the crystalized mutagenic positron element that forms in the rock. Quad magnetism (created by collapsing a Q-star) is the only force that can shield anti-matter in Normal Space. It allows both matter and anti-matter to co-exist. The resulting ante-matter crystals may be a third form of matter between matter and anti-matter. The positron element held large amounts of stabilized anti-quarks. These anti-quarks could produce massively powerful radiation annihilation when collided with normal matter. The further ante-matter gets from a well, the greater the entropy inversion that it possesses and the greater the space-warping effects it can have. In theory this would allow them to produce energy far beyond the biggest fission reactors. Just six pounds of ante-matter crystals could power a planet sized level 7 civilization for three centuries. Ante-matter might even be able to re-start a failing star’s fusion reactions. 

If the node point is damaged the ‘planet’s’ surface will begin to be infected and turned into chemically reactive elements unknown in Normal Space. Bodies of water will turn into a powerful acid. Any lifeform that touches the water will be overwhelmed with pain and die with blisters appearing all over their bodies. The strength of will coming from the anti-matter universe can be physicalized into organic matter. As the infection spreads a red weed, sometimes known as the Red Death, will begin springing up everywhere. The weed will even manifest on the surface of rocks. This ante-matter exposure can eventually manifest as a maroon colored jungle, will be physicalized. Lower lifeforms, such as grey jellyfish that are 10 meters across, can appear. The corrupted weeds and other life is capable of slowly feeding on the life-force of others, eventually destroying their souls. As with almost all things vampiric, the direct light of a sun will repel and weaken any anti-energies.

Part of the purpose of the artificial planet is to monitor the node point, in case the Yssgaroth should try to return. As part of this, in the advent of the crystallized ante-matter being exposed by the actions of a sapient life-form the positron material will disappear during the night when not being observed.

A Black Castle was often placed on the surface of the forced-matter shell, over the node point, to mark its location to the Gallifreyans. This Gallifreyan technology was designed to try to slow the spread of any breach by freezing in time everything that is contaminated. Anything that enters this stasis field will be instantly trapped. But this only slows the infection and, in less than a week, it can spread over dozens of miles of territory.  These castles were constructed out of slabs of dark-black material that have the qualities of rock, concrete, metal, and plastic. The architecture is similar to the medieval castles built on the Earth, complete with gargoyles. The Seal of Rassilon can also be found carved into the walls at regular intervals. The entrance to the castle is sealed by an over 10-meter-tall door. This door is made of a silvery metal and impervious to most physical attacks. The corridors are laid out in an elaborate interlaced pattern. The inside of the Black Castle is decorated with yellow flags and banners with Seals or Rassilon woven into them with gold thread. The air is moist and smells damp. A corkscrew staircase leads down to a dungeon capable of incarcerating and torturing a Great One. The pentagonal courtyard at the exact center of the castle has circle of pillars with a massively deep well reaching all the way down to the HEM sheath. It is known as a Well of the Souls.

A massive power source is needed to make a deep enough breach in a node point that an Yssgaroth could escape. Even then, it takes special technology and rituals on both sides of the hole for a Great One to enter Normal Space. 

Once such a breach occured, it would take the detonation of a nuclear explosion powerful enough to kill all life in a star system to re-seal a breeched plug. If the damaged node point is fully sealed, any Yssgaroth that that hadn't fully manifested would be banished back to its dark universe.
